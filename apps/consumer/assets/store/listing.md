## Full description (4000 max)

Campus Cart NITH brings every shop inside NIT Hamirpur to your phone. Order snacks, meals, juice, stationery and printouts from the canteens and booths on campus, and get them delivered to your hostel or pick them up at the counter.

FOR STUDENTS
• Every campus shop in one place: see which shops are open now and how soon your order will be ready.
• One cart, many shops: add items from different shops and check out once. Each shop gets its own order, and you pay the delivery fee only once.
• Delivery to your hostel or department drop point, or pickup with a token number.
• Schedule an order for later, for example dinner at 8:30 PM.
• Order together: start a group order for your wing, everyone adds their own items, one delivery, and the app works out each person's share.
• Print Store: send a PDF, get it printed and delivered. Only the shop can see your file, and it is deleted a day after your order.
• Live order status, a delivery code for safe handover, and a live map while your order is on its way.
• Ratings, favourites, buy again, and back-in-stock alerts.
• Pay at handover in cash or by UPI.

FOR CAMPUS SHOPS
• Sign in as a shop and take orders with a loud new-order alarm, so nothing gets missed at a busy counter.
• Accept or reject orders, set preparation time, and turn on rush mode when the queue is long.
• Edit your menu and prices, take product photos, mark items out of stock, and restock by scanning barcodes.
• Add counter staff, who take orders but never see your sales.
• See weekly and monthly sales, day by day, and share them as a spreadsheet.

DELIVER FOR CAMPUSCART
Students can sign up as riders, deliver orders to hostels between classes, and get paid weekly by UPI.

Sign in with your @nith.ac.in Google account. Shops sign in with the ID and password from the CampusCart team.

Campus Cart NITH is made for the NIT Hamirpur campus community. It is an independent service and is not run by or affiliated with NIT Hamirpur. It shows no ads and does not sell your data.
