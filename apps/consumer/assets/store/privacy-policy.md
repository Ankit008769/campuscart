# Campus Cart NITH: Privacy Policy

Effective 30 October 2026.

Campus Cart NITH ("CampusCart", "we") lets students of NIT Hamirpur order from campus shops, lets shops take those orders, and lets students deliver them as riders. It is published on Google Play by Dotasara Agrotech. This policy explains what the app collects, why, who sees it, how long it is kept and how to delete it.

We do not sell your data, and we do not show ads.

## What we collect

**When you sign in with Google:** your name and email address.

**What you add to your profile:** your mobile number, your usual hostel or drop point, and a room note (for example "Room 214").

**When you order:** the shop, items, sizes, prices, totals, coupon used, delivery or pickup, the time you ordered and how the order went (accepted, ready, delivered and so on), plus the delivery code and pickup token for each order. Payment happens in person (cash or UPI at handover); the app does not collect card, bank or UPI details from students.

**What you choose to share:** ratings and comments on orders, favourite items, back-in-stock requests, and group orders you start or join.

**Print Store:** PDF files you upload to be printed. A file is deleted automatically 24 hours after its order ends, or a day after upload if it was never ordered.

**Riders:** your UPI ID for weekly payouts, whether you are online, and your deliveries and earnings. While you deliver an order and the delivery screen is open, your phone's location is sent live to that order's student so they can follow it on a map. Location is never stored, is never collected in the background, and stops when the order is delivered.

**Shops:** the names and emails of shop owners and counter staff, the shop's menu, and the product photos the shop uploads.

**How the app is used:** which steps of ordering were reached (app opened, a shop viewed, an item added, checkout opened), with the shop and the time. Search words are kept without any record of who searched.

**Notifications:** a push token for your phone, so we can tell you about your orders. It is removed when you sign out.

## How we use it

- To take, prepare, deliver and hand over orders, and to show you their status.
- To let shops and riders contact you about an order (for example if an item runs out).
- To send order notifications, and occasional campus offers (at most 3 a day).
- To pay riders and to keep shop sales records.
- To prevent fraud and misuse (for example limits on unpaid open orders) and to keep the service safe.
- To understand, in aggregate, where ordering gets stuck, so we can improve the app.

## Who can see your data

- **The shop you order from** sees your name, mobile number, drop point and room note while the order is open and for 7 days after it ends.
- **The rider delivering your order** sees your name, mobile number and drop point only while the delivery is open.
- **Students in a group order** see each other's names only.
- **The CampusCart team** (admins) can see orders and accounts to run the service and resolve problems.
- **Service providers** that run parts of the app for us: Supabase (database and storage, hosted in Mumbai, India), Google (sign-in and Google Play), Expo and Firebase Cloud Messaging (push notifications) and OpenFreeMap (map tiles; your phone requests map images from them).

We share data with authorities only when the law requires it.

## How long we keep it

- Your profile, while your account is open.
- Order records (items, totals, times, the shop and your name and email on them) for up to 3 years, for shop accounting, refunds and disputes.
- Notification messages for 7 days.
- Print Store files for 24 hours after the order ends.
- Live rider location is not kept at all.

## Your choices and rights

- Change your name, mobile number, hostel and room note any time in the app (Account → Your profile).
- Turn notifications off in your phone's settings.
- **Delete your account** in the app (Account → Your profile → Delete my account) or by emailing us; see the account deletion page. Your mobile number, hostel and room note are erased at once and the account is closed. Order records stay as described above.
- You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to erase it, under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, by emailing SUPPORT_EMAIL.

## Security

Data travels encrypted (HTTPS). Every table has access rules in the database itself, so each person reaches only their own data, their shop's orders or their deliveries. Staff access is limited to what they need.

## Age

Campus Cart NITH is meant for students and staff of NIT Hamirpur aged 18 or over. If you are under 18, use it only with your parent's or guardian's consent.

## Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its effective date, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
